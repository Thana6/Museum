Discover Our Heritage

Where History Meets the Horizon

Housed within the historic Batticaloa Fort — built by the Portuguese in 1628 and later fortified by the Dutch — the museum stands as a guardian of Eastern Sri Lanka's rich cultural tapestry. Our collection spans over 10,000 artifacts documenting the region's journey from prehistoric settlements to the modern era.

From ancient palm leaf manuscripts to colonial-era cannons, from the legendary singing fish of the lagoon to the vibrant traditions of Tamil, Muslim, Sinhala, and Burgher communities — every exhibit tells a story of resilience, diversity, and enduring beauty.
